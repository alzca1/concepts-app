= Study mode

The study page runs an active-recall session over the whole deck.

== Session rules

* The deck starts **shuffled** (Fisher–Yates, `common/utils/shuffle`).
* The big card flips on click/keyboard, controlled by the page
  state; see xref:flash-card.adoc[flash card].
* The answer buttons («La tengo clara» / «Volver a ver») appear
  **1500 ms after the flip** (`STUDY_ACTIONS_DELAY`): the container
  reserves its space from the flip and the buttons fade in, staying
  out of the tab order while invisible. Going back to the question,
  answering or restarting cancels the pending timer.
* *La tengo clara* → the card is marked as cleared and leaves the
  deck.
* *Volver a ver* → the card is **requeued at the end** of the deck
  and is not counted as cleared.
* The session ends when every card has been cleared at least once.

== Summary

When the deck is empty the page shows:

* Total visits, repetitions and mastery percentage
  (cleared / visits).
* Actions: restart the session (new shuffle) or go back to the
  cards.

Statistics are per-session only; nothing is persisted between
sessions.
